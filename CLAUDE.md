# Austin Inspection Practice Test

A demo MVP for a job application: a mobile-first self-inspection tool that scores a restaurant against
Austin's 100-point inspection rubric and gives back a ranked fix-list.

## Start of every session
1. Read `PROGRESS.md`. It holds current status, decisions made, and the next task.
2. The original product brief is `resources/austin-inspection-practice-test-plan.md`. Its scope and non-goals still apply.
3. Source documents (official DSHS PDFs plus `pdftotext -layout` text versions) are in `resources/sources/`.

## End of every work chunk
Update `PROGRESS.md`: tick finished items, add any decisions to the Decisions log, and rewrite "Next up"
so a fresh session can continue without this conversation.

## Priorities
Looks polished, works well one-handed on a phone, is accurate to the source form, and is demoable in under a minute.
